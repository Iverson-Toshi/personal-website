HOW THE INTERNET OF THINGS WORKS

Understanding how connected devices communicate.

INTERNET OF THINGS

IoT connects physical devices to the internet, allowing them to collect and exchange information.

01

Devices and Sensors

Sensors collect information such as temperature, humidity, and movement.

02

Connectivity

Devices transmit collected information through Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or other networks.

03

Data Processing

A computer or cloud server processes and analyzes the collected information.

04

User Interface

Users view information and monitor their connected devices through an application.
