My Front-End Development

My journey in learning Front-End Development.

01. Front-End Development

Experience

From what I understand in front-end development, it is what users are able to see and interact with in their web browser. This includes layouts, text, buttons, images, and drop-down menus. For my experience making the Front-End was frustrating at first but although it took time, I was able to make it with ease. The only problem would be memorizing some complicated codes. I think I would prefer this as a career because I am able to showcase my love for designing.

02. Back-End Development

Experience

Back-end development involves the server-side logic and integration of web applications. My experience with back-end development has been challenging but rewarding. I have worked with various programming languages and frameworks to build robust and scalable applications.
